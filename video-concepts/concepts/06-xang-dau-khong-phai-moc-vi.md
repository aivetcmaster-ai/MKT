# Concept 06. Thanh toán xăng dầu. "Đổ xong, không phải móc ví"

Dịch vụ: thanh toán xăng dầu qua tài khoản giao thông.
Đối tượng: tài xế dịch vụ, chủ xe kinh doanh vận tải.
Kênh: TikTok, Reels, Zalo. Tỉ lệ 9:16.
Thời lượng: 12 giây, 2 shot.

## Thông số đặt trong Flow

- Chế độ: Text to Video
- Tỉ lệ: 9:16
- Trạm xăng để trung tính, không logo, không màu nhận diện của đơn vị nào

## Shot 1 (6 giây)

```
Close shot at a fuel station in daytime. A station attendant in a plain
uniform pulls the fuel nozzle out of a car's filler neck and hangs it back
on the pump. The pump display is out of focus and unreadable.
Camera: close on the nozzle and the filler neck, 85mm lens, shallow depth
of field, slight handheld movement.
Lighting: bright overcast daylight under the station canopy, no harsh shadows.
Audio: the nozzle clunking into its holder, pump motor stopping, traffic nearby.
Photorealistic, 24fps, no brand marks anywhere in frame.
```

## Shot 2 (6 giây)

```
The driver, a Vietnamese man in his thirties, leans slightly out of the
window and holds up his phone toward the attendant. The attendant glances
at the screen and nods. The driver puts the phone down, no wallet, no cash,
no card. The car pulls away out of the station.
Camera: medium shot from outside the driver window, then a static wide shot
as the car leaves, 35mm lens.
Lighting: same overcast daylight.
Audio: a short confirmation tone, engine starting, tyres on the forecourt.
Photorealistic, 24fps, no readable text on the phone screen.
```
