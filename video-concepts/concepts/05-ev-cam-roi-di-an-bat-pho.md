# Concept 05. Sạc EV. "Cắm rồi đi ăn bát phở"

Dịch vụ: sạc xe điện.
Đối tượng: chủ xe điện, tài xế dịch vụ chạy xe điện.
Kênh: TikTok, Reels. Tỉ lệ 9:16.
Thời lượng: 15 giây, 2 shot.
Giọng: kênh gần gũi, nhịp nhanh.

## Thông số đặt trong Flow

- Chế độ: Text to Video
- Tỉ lệ: 9:16
- Shot 2 cần timelapse, ghi rõ trong prompt, Veo xử lý timelapse khá tốt

## Shot 1 (7 giây)

```
Evening in a Vietnamese city. A white electric car pulls into a roadside
charging bay. A man in a short sleeve shirt steps out, lifts the charging
connector from the unbranded charging post and plugs it into the car's side
port. A green indicator light starts to glow and reflects on his hand and
on the wet asphalt.
Camera: medium shot behind his shoulder, then close on the connector locking in,
35mm then 85mm lens.
Lighting: blue evening sky, warm street lamps, green glow from the indicator.
Audio: a solid mechanical click, street traffic, a motorbike passing.
Photorealistic, 24fps, no brand marks on the charging post.
```

## Shot 2 (8 giây)

```
Time-lapse from a fixed camera: the same man sits at a small plastic table
at a street food stall across from the charging bay, eating a bowl of pho.
People and motorbikes streak past around him while he stays mostly still.
The time-lapse ends, normal speed resumes, he walks back, unplugs the car
and drives away.
Camera: static wide shot that holds the stall and the car in one frame,
24mm lens, deep focus.
Lighting: warm hanging bulbs over the stall, cool blue street behind.
Audio: street ambience rising and falling, bowl and chopstick sounds, car door.
Photorealistic, 24fps, no text on screen.
```
