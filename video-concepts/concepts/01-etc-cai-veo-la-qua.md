# Concept 01. ETC. "Cái vèo là qua"

Dịch vụ: thu phí điện tử không dừng.
Đối tượng: tài xế cá nhân, xe con.
Kênh: TikTok, Reels, Shorts. Tỉ lệ 9:16.
Thời lượng: 15 giây, 2 shot.
Giọng: kênh gần gũi.

## Thông số đặt trong Flow

- Chế độ: Text to Video
- Tỉ lệ: 9:16
- Số shot: 2, ghép bằng Scene Builder
- Khoá nhất quán: sau khi có shot 1 đạt, dùng frame cuối của shot 1 làm ảnh đầu cho shot 2 qua chế độ Frames to Video

## Shot 1 (8 giây)

```
Over-the-shoulder shot from the passenger seat of a clean modern silver sedan,
early morning. A Vietnamese man in his late thirties, plain grey polo shirt,
hands relaxed on the steering wheel. Through the windshield a Vietnamese
highway toll gantry comes closer, concrete lanes, overhead steel structure,
low golden sunlight flaring softly across the glass. The car does not slow down.
Camera: handheld, slight natural sway, 35mm lens, shallow depth of field,
focus on the driver's hands then racking to the road ahead.
Lighting: warm 6am sunlight from camera left, soft interior fill.
Audio: steady road noise, faint air conditioning hum, no music.
Photorealistic, documentary feel, 24fps.
```

## Shot 2 (7 giây)

```
Macro shot of a small plain white RFID sticker tag on the inside of a car
windshield, top centre, no readable text on it. A red scanner light sweeps
across it for a fraction of a second. Cut wider: the toll barrier arm lifts
smoothly and the silver sedan rolls through the lane without stopping,
seen from a low fixed camera beside the lane.
Camera: macro 100mm for the tag, then a static low wide shot at ground level.
Lighting: same warm morning light, slight haze.
Audio: one short clean electronic beep, barrier motor whirr, tyres on concrete.
Photorealistic, 24fps, no text on screen.
```
