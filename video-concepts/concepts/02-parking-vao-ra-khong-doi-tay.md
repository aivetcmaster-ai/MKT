# Concept 02. VETC Parking. "Vào ra không cần đổi tay"

Dịch vụ: VETC Parking.
Đối tượng: cư dân đô thị, chủ xe đi trung tâm thương mại và sân bay.
Kênh: TikTok, Reels, Facebook. Tỉ lệ 9:16. Bản 16:9 dùng cho màn hình bãi đỗ.
Thời lượng: 20 giây, 3 shot.
Cấu trúc: so sánh trước sau.

## Thông số đặt trong Flow

- Chế độ: Text to Video cho shot 1 và 2, Frames to Video cho shot 3
- Tỉ lệ: 9:16
- Giữ nguyên màu xe và áo người lái qua cả 3 shot

## Shot 1 (6 giây). Cảnh cũ

```
A hand reaches out of a car window and passes a small paper ticket to an
attendant inside a glass booth at the entrance of an underground parking
garage. Behind the car, two more cars wait in line, brake lights on.
Fluorescent ceiling light, slightly green cast, concrete ramp.
Camera: static medium shot from the side, 50mm lens, slight vignette.
Lighting: flat overhead fluorescent, cool and unflattering.
Audio: idling engines, a faint impatient horn far away, no music.
Photorealistic, 24fps, mild sense of friction and waiting.
```

## Shot 2 (7 giây). Cảnh mới

```
The same silver car approaches a different parking entrance. A slim camera
unit mounted above the lane turns toward the car. The boom barrier lifts
immediately and the car drives straight in without stopping and without any
window opening. No queue behind it.
Camera: slow dolly in from behind the car at bumper height, 35mm lens.
Lighting: clean bright white LED, warm ambient spill from the street outside.
Audio: a single soft confirmation tone, barrier motor, tyres on smooth concrete.
Photorealistic, 24fps, calm and effortless mood.
```

## Shot 3 (7 giây)

```
Interior of the car, close on a smartphone resting face up in the centre
console cup holder. The screen lights up briefly with a plain notification
card, no readable text, a small green accent. The driver's hand stays on the
wheel and does not reach for the phone. The car continues down the ramp.
Camera: top down close shot on the phone, then tilt up to the driver's calm
profile, 35mm lens, shallow depth of field.
Lighting: screen glow on the dashboard, warm ramp lighting behind.
Audio: one quiet phone chime, soft engine, no music.
Photorealistic, 24fps, no text on screen.
```

## Hậu kỳ

- Chữ gắn hậu kỳ cho shot 3: thẻ thông báo giả lập, chỉ ghi "Đã thanh toán", không ghi số tiền, không ghi biển số.
- Chữ chạy cuối: "Vào ra không cần đổi tay" rồi sub-logo VETC Parking.
