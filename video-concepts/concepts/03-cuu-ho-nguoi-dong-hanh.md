# Concept 03. Cứu hộ đường bộ. "Trên đường không có ai đi một mình"

Dịch vụ: cứu hộ đường bộ.
Đối tượng: tài xế đường dài, gia đình đi xa.
Kênh: YouTube, Facebook. Tỉ lệ 16:9. Bản cắt 9:16 cho Shorts.
Thời lượng: 20 giây, 3 shot.
Giọng: chính thống, cảm xúc. Đây là concept thương hiệu mạnh nhất trong bộ.

## Thông số đặt trong Flow

- Chế độ: Ingredients to Video, nạp một ảnh nhân vật và một ảnh xe để khoá nhận diện qua 3 shot
- Tỉ lệ: 16:9
- Tông màu: chiều muộn, xanh lam lạnh dần chuyển sang ánh đèn vàng ở shot 3

## Shot 1 (7 giây)

```
A dark blue family SUV stopped on the shoulder of a Vietnamese national
highway at late dusk, hazard lights blinking slowly. A Vietnamese man in his
forties stands beside the open driver door, one hand on the roof, looking
down the empty road into the distance. Rice fields and a line of electricity
poles recede behind him. No other cars.
Camera: wide static shot from across the road, 24mm lens, the car small in
the frame against a big sky.
Lighting: deep blue hour, only the orange hazard lights pulsing.
Audio: distant wind, crickets, the soft click of the hazard relay, no music.
Photorealistic, cinematic but restrained, 24fps.
```

## Shot 2 (6 giây)

```
Close shot of the same man's hands holding a smartphone, screen bright
against the dark. His thumb presses a single large button on the screen.
The blurred asphalt and blinking amber light fill the background.
Camera: close over-the-shoulder, 50mm lens, very shallow depth of field,
focus locked on the thumb and the screen edge.
Lighting: cold screen glow on his face from below, amber rim light behind.
Audio: a single press tone, wind, no music.
Photorealistic, 24fps, no readable text on the screen.
```

## Shot 3 (7 giây)

```
A plain white tow truck with amber beacon lights arrives and stops in front
of the SUV. Its rotating beacon sweeps warm light across the waiting man's
face. A technician in a plain work uniform steps down. The two men exchange
a short nod. No handshake, no dialogue, no smiling at camera.
Camera: medium shot, slow push in, 35mm lens, ends on the man's face as the
tension leaves it.
Lighting: amber beacon sweeping, headlights raking the road surface.
Audio: truck engine settling, door closing, footsteps on gravel.
Photorealistic, 24fps, quiet relief, no music until the last second.
```
